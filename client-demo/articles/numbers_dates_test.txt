The historical society said Tuesday that its oldest building, built in 1605, will reopen to the public on November 5 after a restoration that began in 2013.

Tickets cost $2,600 for a lifetime membership, or $25 for a single visit. The society expects 2,600 visitors in the first month.

Morning hosts at Kiss 108 announced the reopening on air, and Magic 106.7 will broadcast live from the front steps. Listeners can also tune in to 98.5 FM for updates.

For reservations, call (617) 798-0874 or 617-555-0100. Group tours run from Jan. 23-25, 2026, and again on the 5th of March.

“We’ve waited since 1900 for this,” said the society’s director. “On 3 May we’ll celebrate with a party.”

Volunteers tallied the votes for a new logo on Monday. Tallied results favored a distinctive-yet-easily remembered design, which organizers said would boost the town’s self-esteem. The well-known artist behind it will re-enter the building for a follow-up visit next week.
