Natick’s town library said Tuesday that a three-way trivia rivalry between Gen Z, Gen Y’ers and Gen Xers has taken over its weekly game night, and staff are no longer sure who is in charge.

The trouble started when a team of Gen X’ers accused Gen Z’s captain of looking up answers on a phone. The captain, a high school senior with straight A’s, said she simply knew that NVIDIA’s first graphics chip came out in the 1990s.

“I’m not saying they cheated,” said one longtime Gen Y'ers player. “But nobody under 25 should know that many Celtics’ championship years.”

Librarian Dana Ortiz said the Gen Xers have since demanded a rule that every question be answered without a smartphone, a calculator, or “whatever an app is.” Gen Z’s team responded by asking for a question about TikTok in every round.

Hers was the deciding vote, Ortiz said, and she chose a compromise: one round of 1980s movie quotes, one round of internet slang, and one round about Lenox’s history that nobody has yet answered correctly.

The next game night is scheduled for Thursday. Library officials said snacks will be provided, but only to teams that stop arguing about which generation invented the group chat.
